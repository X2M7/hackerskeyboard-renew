Privacy Policy

Hackers Keyboard Renew respects your privacy. This app is designed to work locally on your device.

← Back to Home

Data Collection

The app does NOT:

  • collect personal information
  • collect keyboard input
  • collect passwords
  • collect clipboard content
  • upload any user data
  • use telemetry
  • use analytics
  • use advertising SDKs
  • track users

All keyboard input is processed locally on your device.

Network Access

The app does not transmit typing data or personal information to any server.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
