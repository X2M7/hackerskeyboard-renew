Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Hackers Keyboard Renew ("the app") respects your privacy.

Data Collection

The app does NOT:

  • collect personal information
  • collect keyboard input
  • collect passwords
  • collect clipboard content
  • upload any user data
  • use telemetry
  • use analytics
  • use advertising SDKs
  • track users

All keyboard input is processed locally on your device.

Network Access

The app does not transmit any typing data or personal information to any server.

If future versions introduce optional online features, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
